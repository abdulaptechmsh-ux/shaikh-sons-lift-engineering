Standard Civil Foundation Protocol

Shaikh Sons hoists require vertical boring and a reinforced concrete casing pit. Our nationwide technical engineers provide comprehensive site drawings or end-to-end installation oversight.

01 Civil Stage 1

Vertical Rotary Bore Hole

Execute a 600mm diameter vertical pit bore down to 2,500 mm depth below finished floor level. Ensure 100% true plumb verticality to guarantee frictionless stroke travel.

Diameter: 600 mm (24 Inches)

Depth: 2,500 mm (8.2 Feet)

check_circle Bore rig support available
02 Civil Stage 2

Cylinder Lowering & Concrete Puddle

Shaikh Sons heavy casing cylinder is lowered using a crane hoist. Pour 1:2:4 high-grade concrete mix around outer cylinder with water-repellent additive to seal against groundwater.

Concrete Class: C25/30 Heavy Mix

Curing Period: 72 Hours Mandatory

check_circle Laser level cross-checked
03 Civil Stage 3

Air Trenching & Ramp Coupling

Connect the heavy hydraulic oil line and 3/4-inch schedule 40 pneumatic pipe to wall-mounted operator controller. Mount and torque girder platform with high-tensile hardware.

Airline Dimension: 3/4" High Flow Galvanized

Control Console: 3-Position Rotary Valve
