Service Station Questions

What size air compressor is required?

Any standard single-stage 2HP or 3HP commercial air compressor delivering at least 4 BAR pressure is sufficient to charge and run the 50L foam vessel continuously.

Can this machine handle acid or caustic chemicals?

The Grade 304 stainless steel vessel and internal PTFE seals are fully resistant to high-pH alkaline snow foams and standard car wash cleansers. Extreme hydrofluoric acid tire cleaners are not recommended.

How does nationwide courier freight work?

Your crated machine is booked within 3 hours through our logistics partners (TCS Freight, Daewoo Cargo, or Leopard Cargo) with terminal pickup or door-delivery direct to your station.
